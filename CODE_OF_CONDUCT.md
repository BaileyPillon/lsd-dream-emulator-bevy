# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Keep collaboration respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

- Discuss technical claims using evidence and reproducible examples.
- Credit other people's research, code, and contributions.
- Give feedback on the contribution and help people understand the reasoning.
- Respect privacy and the boundaries around original assets and credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and spam are
not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate to the conduct
and protect productive collaboration. Do not publish personal information
when raising a concern.
